# Tap Race

PlayDen's first game: a quick warm-up to try the room, teams and settings.

**Goal:** get your team to the target first.

**Playing:** tap as fast as you can. Every tap adds 1 to your team. You see your own team's count, and only whether you're ahead or behind the other team.

**Winning:** the first team to the target wins at once. If time runs out, the higher count wins; equal counts are a draw.
